# Solar Fire X Study Plan

A structured route through every chapter of the [Solar Fire X User Guide](https://alabe.com/SFXManual/index.html), ordered for astrologers who already know traditional technique and want to learn the software thoroughly.

**Open the plan:** https://aamanlamba.github.io/solar-fire-x-study/

## What's in it

21 modules in seven phases, about eight weeks at three sessions a week:

1. **Foundations** – a traditional settings baseline, interface fluency, data entry, chart files and collections
2. **The Classical Toolkit** – points, aspects and orbs; dignities, almutens, rulerships and hylegs; reports
3. **Time and Prediction** – subsidiary charts, primary directions and dynamic reports, ephemerides, calendars, eclipses, animation
4. **The Sky Itself** – the full speculum and in mundo work, parans, fixed stars, lots, asteroids, planetarium
5. **Horary, Election and Search** – chart search and electional search
6. **Presentation and Customization** – wheel and page design, printing, interpretations and the compiler
7. **Specialist Arenas** – Solar Maps (astrocartography), symmetrical dials, practice tools, and a capstone

Each module has a short rationale, a hands-on lab, the relevant manual topics (each linked to its page in the guide) and a notes box.

## Your progress

Tick off labs and topics as you go. Progress and notes are saved in your own browser on the device you use; nothing is sent to a server. Use the same browser to pick up where you left off.
