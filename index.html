Solar Fire X Study Plan

A route through every chapter of the SFX User Guide, ordered for a working traditional astrologer: configure the classical toolkit first, then time-lords and directions, the sky itself, horary and electional search, and finally the specialist modules. Each module has a hands-on lab, links to the guide sections it draws on, and every manual topic with a short explanation and the basic step to try it. Progress fills the dial from 0° Aries, counter-clockwise, as a chart does.
